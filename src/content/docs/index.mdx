## The claim this is built on

Most sellers price against a modelled fee. They take the referral rate from the
fee schedule, add an estimate for fulfilment, and call the difference margin.

Measured against the Finances feed rather than the fee schedule, the real cut on
a cheap unit lands between **33% and 43% of the sale price**, not the ~17.85% the
schedule implies. The gap is made of closing fees, per-unit flat charges, postage
bought through Buy Shipping, and fee VAT. The schedule either states these
separately or does not mention them at all.

Biryar does not model the fee. It reads what Amazon actually took, per SKU, per
order, and derives the break-even from it.

```
break-even price = (landed cost + flat charges) / (1 - measured rate)
```

A floor built on a modelled rate is a guess wearing a number's clothes. The whole
point of this system is to stop guessing.
