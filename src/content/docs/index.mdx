Biryar is a program for people who sell on Amazon. It does the reading and
the arithmetic for three jobs: finding products worth buying, setting
prices, and working out what you really earned. Each job ends in a
decision that is yours to take.

## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Sourcing: what is worth buying" icon="magnifier">
    Biryar reads the shelves of the shops you choose, finds every product
    with a barcode, and looks each one up on Amazon. It keeps the ones that
    would still make money after Amazon's real cut, and shows the evidence
    for each: the shelf price, the Amazon price, the fees, how often it
    sells. [How Sourcing works](/guide/sourcing/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Repricing: what to charge" icon="setting">
    For each listing you turn on, Biryar looks at who else sells it,
    works out the band of prices that is safe, and picks a price inside
    it, with a written reason every time. It can never go below your
    break-even or outside your own minimum and maximum.
    [How Repricing works](/guide/repricing/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Portfolio: what was left" icon="document">
    What each product cost you, what it sold for, what Amazon and the
    carrier took, and what was left. From your own payouts and your own
    supplier invoices, never from a fee table. Where it does not know a
    cost, it leaves profit blank and does not guess.
    [How the Portfolio works](/guide/portfolio/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Still to come" icon="rocket">
    Money Amazon owes you for lost or damaged stock, a stock forecast, tax
    figures and business metrics. They are in Biryar's menu already,
    marked "Soon", and the [reports](/reports/) describe them in the
    system Biryar is built from.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Why it is different

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Real fees, not the fee table">
    Biryar works from what Amazon actually kept from your own sales. On a
    cheap product that is a third of the price or more, far above the
    percentage on the fee table, and a tool that trusts the table calls
    deals profitable that are not.
    [How the fees are measured](/methodology/fees/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Every decision comes with its reason">
    A deal shows its evidence. A price shows why it moved, or why it did
    not. When something stops, Biryar says what stopped it and what to do,
    in plain words.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your keys, your computer">
    You connect your own Amazon account with your own application, and
    Biryar runs on your own machine. A licence is checked there too, so
    nothing depends on a server of ours.
    [Where your data lives](/methodology/your-data/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free sees, Pro acts">
    Every shop and every screen is on the free plan. Pro sends prices to
    Amazon and keeps things running by themselves, for €29 a month.
    [Free and Pro](/plans/).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
