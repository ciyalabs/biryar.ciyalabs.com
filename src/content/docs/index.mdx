Biryar is a set of agents that run on your own computer, each one doing a
part of the work of selling on Amazon, and each one ending in a decision
that is yours to take: buy or skip, reprice or hold. It never acts on your
account by itself. It gathers the evidence, writes down what it would do and
why, and on Pro does the part you have switched on.

## The agents

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Sourcing" icon="magnifier">
    Reads your retailers' shelves, finds every barcode, looks each one up on
    Amazon, and keeps what would make money after Amazon's real cut. You
    watch it read each shop, see why it stopped, and get each deal with its
    evidence: the shelf price, the Amazon price, the fees as measured from
    your own sales, the sales rank, who else sells it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Repricing" icon="setting">
    For each listing you turn on: who else sells it and whether you hold
    the Featured Offer, which band of prices is safe, where in the band to
    sit, and a written reason every time. A learner works out the position
    from your own sales and can never leave the band. On Pro, with sending
    switched on, the price goes to Amazon on the schedule you set.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Portfolio" icon="document">
    What each product cost you, what it sold for, what Amazon and the
    carrier took, and what was left, from your settlements and your supplier
    invoices rather than from a fee table. It leaves profit blank rather
    than guess a cost it does not have.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Coming" icon="rocket">
    Reimbursements Amazon owes you, a stock forecast, tax figures and
    business metrics. They are in the sidebar already, marked as coming,
    and the <a href="/reports/">reports</a> describe them in the system
    Biryar is built from.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## What holds it together

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Fees as measured, never modelled">
    Every agent screens and prices against what Amazon actually took from
    your own settled sales: referral fee, fulfilment, the VAT on those fees,
    postage, as a rate plus a flat amount per unit. On cheap items the real
    cut is a third or more of the price, far above the rate on the fee
    schedule, and a tool that uses the schedule calls deals profitable that
    are not. <a href="/methodology/fees/">How the fees are measured</a>.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nothing changes unasked">
    Sourcing finds and explains; you decide and buy. The repricer sends a
    price only on Pro, only after you switch it on, only for listings you
    turned on, and never outside your own floor and ceiling. Nothing is
    bought, no listing is edited, no message is sent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your keys, your machine">
    You register your own application against your own seller account, and
    Biryar runs on your own computer. There is no Biryar server, so there is
    nowhere for your data to go, and a licence is checked offline by its
    signature.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free sees, Pro acts">
    The free plan shows you everything Biryar would do. Pro does it, for €29
    a month. Fourteen days of Pro to try, no card. <a href="/plans/">Free and
    Pro</a>.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Two commands

```bash
curl -fsSLO https://biryar.ciyalabs.com/get/docker-compose.yml
docker compose up -d
```

Then open [localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000). [Get Biryar](/get/) has
the rest; the [support page](/support/) has the answers.
