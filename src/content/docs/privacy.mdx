## In short

There is no account to make on this site and no form to fill in. It sets
no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, apart from remembering
whether you chose the light or the dark theme, which stays on your own
device.

## Writing to us

If you write to us by email, we use what you send to answer you. The legal
basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message is about a purchase, and
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise. We delete it once your request is dealt with,
unless the law requires us to keep it.

## Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, among them Docker, Amazon, Keepa,
ScraperAPI and Ciya Labs. Nothing is passed to them until you follow a
link; from then on, their own privacy notices apply.

## Your rights

You have the right at any time to:

- see the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
- have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR)
- have your data erased (Art. 17 GDPR)
- have its processing restricted (Art. 18 GDPR)
- receive your data in a portable form (Art. 20 GDPR)
- object to its processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

To use any of them, write to the address above.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory
authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live, where you
work, or where you think the law was broken (Art. 77 GDPR).
