**You do not need Keepa for release 0.1.**

Release 0.1 reads your own Amazon account and nothing else. Keepa supplies price
and rank history for products you do not yet own, which matters for sourcing
decisions rather than for understanding what you already sell. Sourcing is
deliberately out of 0.1.

This page exists so the answer to "do I need to buy a Keepa subscription before I
start?" is findable, and the answer is no.

## When sourcing arrives

You will supply your own Keepa key the same way you supply your own Amazon keys.
The token cost will be yours rather than resold, which also means your sourcing
scans are limited by your own plan and not by someone else's quota.

The [Sourcing report](/reports/sourcing/) describes what that part of the system
does, if you want to judge it before it ships.
