be read. The free plan needs no purchase and is not covered here.

## Who can buy

Biryar Pro is sold to businesses: people and companies who buy it for
their trade, business or profession. It is not offered to consumers. By
buying, you confirm that you buy as a business.

## What you buy

A licence for the Pro plan of Biryar, for one business, with as many
people in it as you like, for one month or for one year.

- The licence is one line of text, sent by email.
- The term starts on the day the licence is sent. If you renew while a
  licence is still running, the new one starts the day the old one ends.
- Biryar runs on your own computer. We do not run it for you and hold
  none of your data.
- When a licence ends, Biryar carries on with the free plan and nothing
  of yours is deleted.

What Pro includes is described on [Free and Pro](/plans/).

## Price and payment

- One month: €29. One year: €290.
- No VAT is charged: we are a small business under § 19 of the German
  VAT Act (UStG). The invoice says so.
- You pay by card or direct debit on a payment page run by Stripe, or by
  bank transfer against an invoice, payable within 14 days.
- You receive an invoice by email for every purchase.

## Delivery

The licence is sent by email as soon as we see your payment, and at the
latest on the next working day. A direct debit or a bank transfer takes a
few days to arrive; the licence follows when it has.

## Nothing renews by itself

Every purchase is a single payment for a fixed term. Nothing is charged
again unless you buy again. About two weeks before a licence ends, Biryar
says so on its Home page and we write to you.

## Refunds

**Within 14 days of paying, you get your money back in full, without
giving a reason.** Write to
[info@ciyalabs.com](mailto:info@ciyalabs.com?subject=Biryar%20refund) from
the address you bought with, or name the invoice number.

- The money goes back the way it came, within 14 days of your message.
- The licence ends with the refund. You may no longer use it, and Biryar
  carries on with the free plan.
- After 14 days there is no refund for the time that is left, since
  nothing renews and you can simply not buy again.
- If we cannot deliver what you paid for, you get your money back
  whenever that turns out.

Every business can also try Pro for fourteen days without paying, under
**Settings, Plan**, before buying anything.

## What the licence allows

You may use Biryar Pro in the one business the licence names, on as many
computers of that business as you need. You may not pass the licence on,
resell it or publish it.

## What Biryar does, and what stays yours

Biryar reads your Amazon account, shops you choose, and services you hold
your own accounts with, and it suggests decisions. Some of them it carries
out when you switch that on: on Pro it can send prices to Amazon within
the minimum and maximum you set.

- **The decisions stay yours.** What you buy, what you charge and what
  limits you set are your business decisions. Check what Biryar shows
  before you act on it.
- **Other services are not ours.** Amazon, Keepa, ScraperAPI and the
  shops can change what they offer or how they answer, and a part of
  Biryar may then stop working until we have adapted it. Their fees and
  their terms are between you and them.
- **Updates and help are included** for as long as your licence runs. We
  answer by email, normally within two working days.
