Selling on Amazon comes with its own vocabulary, and so does any program.
These are the words this site and Biryar's screens use, in the order you
are likely to meet them.

## Amazon's words

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Seller Central** | Amazon's website for sellers, where you manage listings, prices and payouts. |
| **Listing** | One product you offer on Amazon, with its price and stock. |
| **SKU** | Your own name or number for a listing. Amazon's records identify your products by it. |
| **ASIN** | Amazon's own number for a product page. Every seller of the same product shares it. |
| **Barcode, EAN, ISBN** | The number under the stripes on a product. It is how a product in a shop is matched to the same product on Amazon. |
| **Featured Offer** | The offer behind the Add to Cart button, also called the Buy Box. When several sellers offer one product, most sales go to whoever holds it. |
| **Minimum and maximum price** | Limits you set for a listing in Seller Central. Amazon refuses a price outside them. |
| **Settlement, statement, payout** | Amazon's record of what your sales brought in, what it kept and what it paid you. |
| **FBA** | Fulfilled by Amazon: your stock is in Amazon's warehouse and Amazon ships it. |
| **Sales rank** | Amazon's ranking of how well a product sells in its category. A smaller number sells more. |

## Biryar's words

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Sourcing** | Looking for products you could buy in a shop and sell on Amazon at a profit. |
| **Retailer** | A shop Biryar can read. |
| **Scan** | One reading of one or more shops, started by you. |
| **Pass** | One round of work that Biryar does by itself on a schedule: a pass over a shop, or a repricing pass over your listings. |
| **Deal** | A product that would make money after the shelf price and Amazon's real cut. |
| **ROI** | Return on investment: the profit as a share of what you paid. 40% means every €10 spent brings €4 on top. |
| **Repricing** | Working out what a listing should cost, and, if you allow it, changing the price on Amazon. |
| **Floor** | The lowest price Biryar will ever choose: the higher of your break-even and your minimum in Seller Central. |
| **Ceiling** | The highest: your maximum in Seller Central. |
| **Band** | The prices between the floor and the ceiling. Biryar never leaves it. |
| **Break-even** | The price at which a sale brings back exactly what the unit cost you, after Amazon's cut. |
| **Learner** | The part of Biryar that learns from your own sales where in the band a price earns most. |
| **Portfolio** | The page that shows, per product, what it cost, what it sold for and what was left. |
| **Buy price, landed cost** | What a unit cost you, including delivery and any VAT you cannot reclaim. |
| **Owner, assistant** | The two kinds of account. An owner can do everything; an assistant can see and prepare, and cannot do what changes money. |

## Installing and keys

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Docker** | A free program that runs other programs in a sealed box. It is the one thing you install before Biryar. |
| **Terminal** | A window you type commands into. On Windows it is called PowerShell. |
| **Setup code** | A code Biryar prints when it first starts. The first person to sign up needs it, so a stranger cannot become the owner. |
| **Two-factor sign-in** | Signing in with your password and a six-digit code from an app on your phone. |
| **Sealing key** | The key, made on your own computer, that locks your Amazon and service keys before they are stored. |
| **Application** | What Amazon calls the visitor's pass you register so that a program may read your account. |
| **Refresh token** | The long text Amazon gives you when you allow your application to read your account. |
| **Keepa** | A service that knows what products sell for on Amazon, and how often. Sourcing needs a Keepa key. |
| **Token** | What Keepa counts in. Looking up one product costs one token, and tokens refill every minute. |
| **ScraperAPI** | A service that reads shops which turn programs away. Only some shops need it. |
| **Credit** | What ScraperAPI counts in. Each page read through it costs one or more. |
| **Licence** | One line of text that switches Pro on. It is checked on your own computer. |
